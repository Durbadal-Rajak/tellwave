Use Tellwave from Claude

Checking…

Connect this site to Claude, then ask for voiceovers and music while you plan or make a video. Claude gets a download link for each file.

  1. Create a secret key

    It goes at the end of your connector address, so only you can use it.

  2. Add it to Vercel

    In your Vercel project, open Settings, then Environment Variables. Add a variable named MCP_SECRET with your key as the value, then redeploy the project.

  3. Add the connector in Claude

    On claude.ai, open Customize, then Connectors. Choose Add, then Add custom connector, name it Tellwave, paste this address and pick No sign in. It then works in the Claude app too.

  4. Ask for something

    In a chat, turn Tellwave on from the + button under Connectors, then ask. For example: “Use Tellwave to make a 45-second suspense track and a voiceover of this intro with a deep male voice.”

Check your key

Paste the key you saved in Vercel to test the connection.

What Claude can do

  • Make voiceovers from a script, with any of the 28 voices, up to four speakers and adjustable speed.
  • Compose music in eight moods, from 5 seconds to 5 minutes. Original, no credit needed.
  • Find free tracks and sound effects, with the credit line when one is required.
  • List voices so it can pick one that suits your video.

Claude's requests run on your Vercel server, which includes about 4 hours of processing a month on the free plan. This website itself runs on your device and has no limit.
